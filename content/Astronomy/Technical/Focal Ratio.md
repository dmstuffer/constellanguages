The focal ratio is the focal length of the telescope divided by its aperture.

A telescope's focal ratio (or f-ratio, e.g., f/5, f/10) determines its "speed" and light-gathering concentration, calculated by dividing the telescope's focal length by its aperture. 

Short - Lower numbers (< f/6) are "fast," offering wider views and shorter exposure times for astrophotography.

Long - Higher numbers (f/10) are "slow," providing higher magnification for planetary viewing.
